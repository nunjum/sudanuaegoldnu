# Strategic Supply Chain Tracing: The Geopolitical Economy of Sudanese Gold

An investigative open-source data repository mapping the extractive corridors of Sudanese gold. This project traces the physical and financial pipelines from artisanal extraction sites in Sudan to global distribution hubs, detailing the associated wealth asymmetry, public health impacts, and institutional accountability.

## 🏛️ Category 1: Extractive Hegemony & Sovereign Shift (1940s–2020s)

*Systemic historical data charting the evolution of gold extraction from colonial mandates to modern war liquidity.*

* **1940s–1950s (Late Colonial Mandate):** Mapping early geological surveys conducted under Anglo-Egyptian rule; institutional frameworks establishing state-centric mineral monopolies.
* **1970s–1980s (State Mining Failures):** Data on failed large-scale state-run mining attempts; economic shifts pushing local populations toward informal survival-driven extraction.
* **2008–2011 (The Great Gold Rush):** Sudden explosion of Artisanal and Small-scale Gold Mining (ASGM) following the secession of South Sudan and loss of 75% of state oil revenues.
* **2019–2023 (Paramilitary Consolidation):** The rise of the Rapid Support Forces (RSF) seizing the Al-Junaid mining concessions in Darfur, turning gold into independent sovereign liquidity outside the Central Bank of Sudan.
* **2023–2026 (Wartime Supply Chain Bifurcation):** Deep tracking of the ongoing civil war where gold serves as the primary currency of conflict:
  * **The Northern & Eastern Route:** Controlled by the Sudanese Armed Forces (SAF), routing bullion through Port Sudan primarily toward Egypt and Saudi Arabia.
  * **The Western & Southern Route:** Controlled by the RSF, illicitly routing gold across borders via Chad, South Sudan, and Central African Republic (CAR) bound for international transit hubs.

## 💎 Category 2: Global End-Products & Transshipment Vectors

*Granular material tracking proving that gold is a critical asset across advanced industries, alongside global exporter datasets.*

### 🔄 The Extraction-to-Molding Lifecycle Matrix

This matrix traces the structural physical transformations of Sudanese gold from localized labor to high-tech industrial integration:

1. **Phase 1: Raw Extraction (Artisanal Pits):** Gold-bearing quartz veins are blasted or dug out manually by laborers in localized hubs (e.g., River Nile State, Blue Nile, Darfur). The ore is crushed mechanically into a fine powder.
2. **Phase 2: Chemical Amalgamation:** Liquid mercury is introduced to the powdered ore by hand, binding to the gold particles to form a silvery metallic amalgam paste. This paste is squeezed through cloths and heated with open blowtorches—vaporizing the mercury into the atmosphere and leaving behind a raw gold sponge.
3. **Phase 3: Local Smelting & Doré Production:** The sponge gold is melted down in basic regional workshops into rough, unrefined bullion bars known as *Doré* (typically containing 80% to 90% pure gold alongside silver and copper impurities).
4. **Phase 4: Border Leakage & Transshipment:** Doré bars are moved across border checkpoints or flown out via cargo arrays out of Port Sudan or porous neighboring airfields, arriving at global smelting/refining networks.
5. **Phase 5: High-Purity Industrial Refining:** Refineries subject the Doré to advanced chemical processing (such as the Wohlwill electrolytic process or Aqua Regia acid dissolution) to strip all secondary metals, producing fine gold bullion certified at **99.99% purity (24 Karat)**.
6. **Phase 6: Industrial Molding into End-Products:**
   * **Bonding Wire Extrusion:** Pure gold is drawn through diamond dies to form microscopic micro-wires (often thinner than a human hair) utilized for microchip circuitry.
   * **Sputtering Targets & Electroplating:** Gold is cast into dense targets used in vacuum chambers to vapor-deposit atomic-scale protective layers onto aerospace visors, satellite thermal blankets, and high-performance fiber optic connectors.
   * **Chemical Nanoparticle Synthesis:** Refined gold is dissolved to create chloroauric acid solutions, producing colloidal gold particles engineered for targeted drug delivery vectors in biotechnology.

### Exporting & Transshipment Country Dataset

* **Primary Transshipment Hubs:** Historic tracking of Dubai (UAE) as the dominant destination absorbing over 90% of official exports up to 2024.
* **The Modern Corridor Shift:** Trade data reflecting a radical pivot due to shifting diplomatic allegiances:
  * **Egypt:** Acting as a major processing and purchase buffer intermediary.
  * **Saudi Arabia & Qatar:** Expanding state-backed mineral concessions, processing networks, and joint exploration programs along the Red Sea coast.

## 💸 Category 3: Financial Concentration & Wealth Asymmetry

*An analysis of capital flight, examining structural imbalances between Gulf capital networks and East African labor.*

* **The Asymmetry Equation:** Tracking the massive disparity where up to 80% of gold output leaves the country un-monetized by domestic institutions via unregulated parallel markets.
* **Capital Diversion vs. Social Safety Nets:** Data showing that captured revenues are weaponized for logistics, weapons procurement, and military financing rather than local economic-social development.

## ☣️ Category 4: Health Ecotoxicology & State Accountability

*Documenting chemical contamination in frontline communities and analyzing international corporate and political exposure.*

### Anthropogenic Health Footprint (Focus: River Nile State & Sudan)

* **The 'Karta' Poisoning Epidemic:** Over 450,000 tons of *karta* (highly toxic mercury-laden mineral tailings) piled directly adjacent to residential zones and agricultural lands north of Atbara.
* **Bioaccumulation In Children & Families:** Medical field surveys reporting extreme urinary mercury concentrations (exceeding 100 μg/L in 98% of exposed subjects), directly causing profound neurological damage, loss of cognitive functions, and motor tremors.
* **Maternal & Environmental Rupture:** Escalating rates of spontaneous miscarriages and congenital birth defects among communities near chemical-leaching basins, alongside the poisoning of vital wheat, fava bean, and livestock systems.

### International Liability & Accountability Tracker

* **The Qatar Conundrum:** Assessing the legal and corporate liability of Qatari state-backed mining enterprises expanding mineral investments (including large-scale copper and gold concessions) during active conflict.
* **Accountability Vector:** Documenting whether these international firms enforce the Minamata Convention on Mercury or actively turn a blind eye to downstream processing lines mixed with local artisanal chemical runoffs.
