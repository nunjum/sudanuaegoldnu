Limits of this data

Declared trade only: hand-carried gold, the main smuggling channel SWISSAID describes, is absent. Counts are records, not weight or value. Top-buyer activity runs through late 2024. Many buyer records lack identifiers, so one business can appear under several names.

Next steps
